# Guandan Studio V0.2

Release: **v0.2.0** · 2026-10-23

[Play / 开始练习](https://djzts.github.io/Guandan-Studio/)

## 中文

V0.2 汇总 V0.1 上线后的规则教学、主页导航和理牌交互改进。

### 新增

- 英文独立规则页：介绍四人搭档、牌型与大小、出牌流程、红心级牌配牌等；重点术语附中文与带声调拼音。
- 网站增加返回 FutureLab 主页的入口。

### 理牌体验修复

- 选中牌移到同点数牌列的前排，未选中的牌移到后排并向上展开，保留每张牌的点数和花色。
- 取消选择后恢复自然排列顺序。
- 组好的牌放在左侧；右侧剩余手牌保持同点数竖叠、不同点数横排。
- 可以连续组多个牌型，例如先组三带二，再组对子；拆组后牌回到剩余手牌区。
- 自动理牌中的未锁定单张归入剩余手牌区，避免单张组合占满横向空间；锁定的单张仍保留独立组。

### 验证与兼容性

- 18 项自动测试通过，涵盖红心配牌、合法牌型、完整模拟对局及新增理牌布局测试。
- 浏览器验证了前后排选牌、牌角无遮挡，以及连续组出三带二和对子后的剩余竖叠布局。
- 版本标签更新为 V0.2；本地存档格式保持兼容，不因版本号更新清除已有练习进度。

### 当前范围

仍为独立单局练习，不含完整升级赛程与进还贡。评分为启发式参考，不是胜率；电脑采用基础策略，自动理牌不保证全局最优。红心级牌继续作为配牌参与合法组合，不能代替大小王。

## English

V0.2 collects the rules guide, navigation improvements, and card-arrangement fixes added after V0.1.

### Added

- A dedicated English rules page covering partnerships, legal patterns, card order, play, and heart-level wild cards. Key concepts include Chinese terms and Mandarin pinyin with tone marks.
- Links back to the FutureLab homepage.

### Arrangement fixes

- Selected cards move to the front of their rank stack. Unselected cards move behind and spread upward so every rank and suit stays visible.
- Deselecting restores the natural card order.
- Completed groups stay on the left. Remaining cards stay on the right, stacked vertically by rank and arranged horizontally across ranks.
- Grouping is repeatable: create a full house, then a pair, and continue. Dismantled groups return to the remaining hand.
- Unlocked singleton groups from automatic arrangements stay in the remaining hand instead of taking up individual group panels. Locked singles remain separate groups.

### Validation and compatibility

- All 18 automated tests pass, covering wild cards, legal patterns, complete simulated deals, and the new arrangement-layout cases.
- Browser checks cover selecting front and rear cards, unobstructed card headers, and consecutive full-house and pair grouping with stacked remaining cards.
- The visible version is V0.2. The saved-deal format remains compatible, preserving existing local practice sessions.

### Current scope

Independent practice deals only; full-match leveling and tribute are not implemented. Scores are heuristic references, not win probabilities. Computer play uses a basic policy, and automatic arrangements are not guaranteed global optima. Heart-level cards remain wild in legal combinations and cannot substitute for jokers.
